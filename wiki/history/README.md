# Wiki History Archive

Updated: 2026-07-09

`wiki/history/` stores documents that are completed, superseded, or useful only as historical decision background.

Current active product and design constraints are not here. They live in:

- [../../PRODUCT.md](../../PRODUCT.md)
- [../../DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md)
- [../../domains/frontend/documents/current-skill-workflow-loop-workbench.md](../../domains/frontend/documents/current-skill-workflow-loop-workbench.md)

## Archive Categories

- `architecture/`: superseded architecture and interaction directions.
- `design/`: old prototypes, screenshots, visual explorations, and design packets.
- `plan/`: completed or superseded implementation plans.
- `prd/`: completed or superseded product requirement documents.
- `qa/`: old QA reports and acceptance records.

## Rule

Archived documents may be used to understand history, examples, or rejected directions. They must not be used as current acceptance criteria unless a future plan explicitly reactivates one file by path.
